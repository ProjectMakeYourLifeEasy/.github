
# Project Make Your Life Easy

**Making everyday life easier — even by 0.1%.**

## About

Project Make Your Life Easy is a community-driven organization for hobby developers, builders, and curious minds who enjoy creating practical projects that solve everyday problems.

Our goal is simple:
If a project can make life **even 0.1% easier**, it is worth building.

## Mission

* Build useful tools and applications
* Solve small, real-world problems
* Share knowledge openly
* Create practical projects that improve daily life

## Who We Are

We are hobby developers passionate about building things that make a tangible difference—big or small.

## Contribute

Ideas, experiments, and contributions are always welcome.

Together, we build small things that make life better.
